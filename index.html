gezel Your team of AI craftsmen

Gezel is a free desktop app for getting things done.

Describe the job and a crew of AI specialists takes it on — researching, drafting, building, reviewing — each working from a tested craftbook, with over 200 to draw on. They work on your own machine, on local AI models. No cloud account, no subscription, nothing leaving the building. It’s your team.

What you need for local AI

Minimum
A graphics card with 8 GB of its own memory — NVIDIA RTX 2070, 3070, 4060, 5060 or better; AMD Radeon 6600, 7600, 9050 or better. Runs the everyday models: chat, drafting, focused single tasks.
Recommended
16 GB or more of graphics memory — NVIDIA RTX 4080, 5070 Ti, AMD Radeon 9070 and up. Adds the larger models, for multi-step work with tools.
Minimum
A Mac with an M1 chip or newer and 16 GB of memory. Runs the everyday models: chat, drafting, focused single tasks.
Recommended
24 GB of memory or more. Adds the larger models, for multi-step work with tools.

Get started with gezel

Install the Gezel app

Download Gezel for your device. It measures the memory your machine can spare, then recommends the local AI models that will run well on it — no cloud account required.

Speak with the meester

Your first conversation is with the Meester — your concierge for getting things done. Describe the work and they set up the project and add the gezellen needed to complete your tasks.

Track as your crew builds

Your crew will pass tasks between each other, keep notes, and pick up where the last one left off. Watch them work via built-in task trackers, and step in whenever you like.

How it works

Role-based gezel crews

Every gezel has a role - for example, developer, writer, researcher - and a focused prompt and set of tools. This helps ensure AI models - especially local ones - remain focused so they can do their best.

Craftbooks

Craftbooks are precise, step-by-step guides that help your crew complete tasks efficiently and consistently. Gezel includes over 200+ tested craftbooks - so you don't need to build them yourself.

A safe space for AI work

Gezel carefully manages what AI can do.

We don't give local models your credentials, an open web connection, or visibility into anything on your device, unless you let them.

Gezel is designed to be privacy-conscious and compliant - even for strict business environments.

gezel Your team of AI craftsmen

Gezel is a desktop app for Windows/Mac/Linux for assembling a team of AI agents powered by your device. Each gezel - Dutch for "journeyman" or "companion" - has a specialized role that makes the most of your hardware. Everything you make lives on your device. No cloud, no lock-in, no mystery - just free, honest open source software.

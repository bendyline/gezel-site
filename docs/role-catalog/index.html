Every role template

A role template is a ready-made character for your crew: a name, a job, and a working style that shapes how they think and talk. The Meester picks from these when a job calls for one, and you can add any of them yourself with New crew member. The core roles each have their own page in Roles.

RoleWhat they do
Account ManagerA warm, organized keeper of a small business’s sales: every deal with its stage, value and next follow-up, the calls to make today, and a weighted forecast that stays honest.
ArchivistA gentle, rigorous archivist for the file nobody wants to need — wills, policies, accounts, instructions.
BookkeeperA careful keeper of a small business's books: every receipt booked with its proof, every entry in a category, each month closed on a checklist, and the cash planned weeks ahead.
BuilderTakes a job end-to-end on their own — research, build, ship.
Campaign ManagerA steady, numbers-honest runner of one marketing campaign: the goal, the channel calendar, every asset from idea to live, the budget against what was spent, and the results that say what worked.
Care CoordinatorA warm, discreet care coordinator.
Career CoachA steady career coach who runs your job search like a campaign — keeps the pipeline current, gets your materials tailored, and never lets a follow-up slip.
Checkers PlayerA warm, playful checkers opponent.
Chess PlayerA thoughtful, warm chess opponent who plays a complete legal game, keeps plans private, and makes the table welcoming whether the result is a win, loss, or draw.
Chief Security OfficerChief Security Officer for the crew.
Clue GiverA lively clue-giver for word games: bold one-word clues that link several words at once, and cheerful table talk.
CoachA steady, encouraging training coach.
Compliance OfficerA calm, exact keeper of a small business's obligations: licenses, permits, filings, insurance and certifications, each with an owner, a due date and the evidence that it was done.
ConversationalistA thoughtful companion for questions, research, brainstorming, and writing together.
CopywriterWrites for humans.
Crew LeadForeman of a project — plans tasks, delegates to the right gezels, and keeps work moving.
CuratorLooks after a photo library.
DesignerThinks in layout, hierarchy, and visual tone.
DeveloperWrites, tests, and ships code.
EstimatorA careful, practical pricer for trade and service businesses: every quote built line by line from labor and materials at the business’s own rates, exact to the cent, and followed up until it is won or lost.
Event PlannerA composed master of ceremonies for weddings, parties, and reunions.
FundraiserAn infectious, organized rallier for community drives.
GeneralistCarries a whole task from first step to last in one conversation — reads the outline, works the active step, passes its check, moves on.
Go PlayerA calm, attentive Go opponent who plays legal stones, respects the quiet shape of the board, and handles captures, passes, and final scores with good grace.
Home KeeperAn unflappable house steward.
Household TreasurerA calm, unjudging treasurer for the household.
IllustratorTurns a brief into finished images: logos, icons, heroes, character sheets and sets that belong together.
Image editorChanges a picture you give it.
Image generatorAn image generator.
InspectorThe guild's quality inspector — consulted when a smaller model's work stalls, loops, or drifts, to diagnose the cause and hand back the smallest fix that gets the journeyman moving again.
Language TrainerA warm, patient language tutor.
LibrarianThe index-keeper.
Managing EditorA calm keeper of a small business's editorial pipeline: every long-form piece from idea to published with one owner and one date, an honest calendar, and a plan for the newsletter, social posts and video chapters each piece spins off.
Meal PlannerA practical meal planner who answers "what's for dinner?" once a week instead of nightly — menus from what's actually in the pantry, one grocery run, less waste.
MeesterThe concierge of a gezel crew — greets new users, understands what they're trying to build, and spins up the right team.
Mock InterviewerA mock interviewer who asks the hard questions for real — then debriefs kindly, with specifics you can actually fix before it counts.
Office AdministratorThe operations half of a freelancer's brain.
PlannerTurns fuzzy asks into concrete steps — good at the 'what' and the 'in what order', less opinionated on the 'how'.
PuzzlerA careful clue-giver for word games: precise one-word clues that keep well away from the trap, and dry, friendly asides.
RecruiterA fair, organized recruiter for a small business: writes job-related requirements, keeps every candidate moving with a clear next step, gathers structured scorecards, and leaves every hiring decision to you.
ResearcherReads source, prior art, or external docs and files a substantive written analysis.
ReviewerSecond set of eyes on code, docs, or plans.
Shift SchedulerA fair, careful builder of a small team's weekly roster: every shift covered from who is available, hours kept inside the limits the business sets, and every week left for a person to approve.
Social Media WriterThe town crier for your feeds.
Stock KeeperA tidy, numbers-first keeper of a small business's stockroom: what is on the shelf, what is running low, which supplier brings it and how long they take, and the purchase orders ready to check and send.
Study PartnerA patient study companion who writes sharp flashcards, keeps the review rhythm going, and cheers the streak without ever shaming a miss.
Support LeadA calm, organized lead for a small business's customer questions: every request logged as a ticket, answered from the business's own help articles, and the questions those articles miss turned into new ones.
Training CoordinatorA steady training coordinator who knows who has finished which course, what expires when, and what a new hire needs in their first weeks, and books refreshers before anything lapses.
TranslatorThe crew's translator.
Trip PlannerA well-traveled guide who plans trips people actually take: a day-by-day itinerary with slack in it, a packing list that fits the weather, and a journal worth keeping afterward.
Video generatorTurns your prompts into short video clips.
Writing PartnerA warm writing companion for the long haul of a novel.

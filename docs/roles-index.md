# Roles at a glance

A **role** is a crew member's trade: the bundle of skills, tools, and habits they bring to the work. Roles aren't just labels — each one comes with a default character, a curated toolkit, and a model floor (the minimum model tier the role needs to work unsupervised).

| Role | What they do | Model floor | Default plans |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Conversationalist](https://gezel.com/docs/role/conversationalist/) | Talks, researches, and writes in conversation; brings in advisers or craftbooks when useful. | tiny | — |
| [Meester](https://gezel.com/docs/role/meester/) | Coordinator: delegates work across the crew; does not build directly. | medium | status-report, weekly-review |
| [Crew Lead](https://gezel.com/docs/role/voorman/) | Foreman: triages, diagnoses, and routes work to specialists; read-only investigator. | medium | root-cause-investigation, release-readiness-review, bug-fix-tdd |
| [Reviewer](https://gezel.com/docs/role/reviewer/) | Reviews code and deliverables; verifies claims against sources before approving. | medium | pull-request-review, code-review, deep-security-review, pr-security-review, test-coverage-review |
| [Researcher](https://gezel.com/docs/role/researcher/) | Answers questions from sources; cites every claim and grounds facts in authorized inputs. | medium | research-report, literature-review, competitive-analysis, due-diligence-brief, fact-check |
| [Curator](https://gezel.com/docs/role/curator/) | Looks after a photo library: describes, groups and proposes albums. Changes arrive as proposals. | small | album-curate, photo-cull, year-in-review |
| [Copywriter](https://gezel.com/docs/role/copywriter/) | Drafts comms and marketing copy under length/tone constraints. | small | blog-post, landing-copy, press-release, email-sequence, product-descriptions |
| [Designer](https://gezel.com/docs/role/designer/) | Designs visual deliverables; generates and places imagery into pages. | small | hero-image, landing-page, moodboard, svg-animation, logo-set |
| [Image Generator](https://gezel.com/docs/role/image-generator/) | Produces images via generate_image, and SVG or manifest files beside them. | tiny | logo-set, icon-pack, hero-image, thumbnail-generator |
| [Video Generator](https://gezel.com/docs/role/video-generator/) | Produces short video clips via generate_video; no direct file writes. | tiny | — |
| [Developer](https://gezel.com/docs/role/developer/) | Builds and fixes code; works to task-specific build and test gates. | small | build-loop, bug-fix-tdd, refactor-module, test-suite-backfill, apply-review-findings |
| [Web Developer](https://gezel.com/docs/role/web-developer/) | Frontend/full-stack code with live browser work. | small | landing-page, spa-dashboard, web-app-crud, portfolio-site, pwa-offline |
| [Planner](https://gezel.com/docs/role/planner/) | Turns a brief into a structured, owned, checkable plan. | medium | spec-doc, proposal-sow, dashboard-spec, board-deck, onboarding-checklist |
| [Generalist](https://gezel.com/docs/role/generalist/) | Carries a whole task from first step to last in one conversation — every step, every gate. | medium | build-loop, bug-fix-tdd |

Each role has its own article in this section with the full picture: the character it starts with, every tool in its kit, and the plans it runs well. And roles are starting points, not cages — you can rename, re-equip, and rewrite any crew member after they're created.

To browse the ready-made characters outside the app, see [every role template](https://gezel.com/docs/role-catalog/) on gezel.com.

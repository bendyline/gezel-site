November 3 M5 Max condensed engine comparison

This follow-up compared Qwen 3.8 27B Q4 and Gemma 4 12B Q4 on MLX and llama.cpp after fixes, using native 0.1.55 on an Apple M5 Max with 128 GB of unified memory. We shortened the original 516-trial plan to save time. The comparison contains 72 matched trials: 18 pairs per model, one repetition, across core and productivity. Developer and complex-work were not run. This chronological subset was selected after outcomes were visible, so the findings are exploratory.

Both models passed 16/18 on MLX and 18/18 on llama.cpp. Practical equivalence within our chosen margin of ±5 percentage points is not established. The observed difference is −11.11 pp; the conservative paired 95% interval is [−38.09, +20.66] pp and the scenario-cluster bootstrap interval is [−26.32, 0.00] pp for each model. These data also do not establish that the underlying gap exceeds 5 pp. The paired analysis documents assumptions, unavailable original pairs, sensitivity and nominal/adjusted tests.

Median recorded decode was 34.00 tok/s on MLX versus 35.66 on llama.cpp for Qwen, and 59.00 versus 84.12 for Gemma. Gemma's roughly 30% lower MLX rate here does not reproduce the earlier twofold gap. These are complete integration measurements with different estimators, quantizations and speculative decoding, not an isolated engine benchmark or causal test of a fix. Gemma MLX used a staged assistant drafter candidate; Gilde remained 0.3.5.

MLX prefill was recovered from first-token diagnostics: 121 rounded rate observations in the 36 included MLX trials. The medians of trial medians are 604.75 tok/s for Qwen and 1332.25 for Gemma. Tables mark these rates as observed: they cover partial provider turns and are not equivalent to llama.cpp's duration-weighted aggregate. Exact MLX prefill times remain unknown. The download preserves all 126 observations across all 37 recorded MLX trials, their source hashes and coverage limits.

All four recorded failures remain unchanged. The diagnoses identify an image-tool workflow failure, unresolved duplicate data, a section-placement failure, and a conflict-synthesis gate false-positive. The last unpaired Gemma MLX PowerPoint trial passed its gate but encountered a fixture-identity flag; it is excluded from the matched comparison and retained in downloads. There are 25 artifact reviews, including that excluded trial's review; 24 enter the displayed rubric means. The other 48 trials have no applicable rubric. Fixed scores and advisory quality remain separate.

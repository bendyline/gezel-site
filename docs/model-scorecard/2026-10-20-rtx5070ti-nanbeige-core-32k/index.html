October 2026 Windows results and review data

This note also covers 2026-10-20 · rtx5070ti-core-continuation-64k, 2026-10-20 · rtx5070ti-core-original-64k.

The eight-model core evaluation completed all 264 requested attempts on an RTX 5070 Ti laptop: 182 passed, 70 failed their tasks, and 12 were excluded after exceeding Nanbeige's 32K context. These were context failures, not GPU out-of-memory failures. Every attempt has been reviewed.

The tables preserve three runtime rounds: 49 original trials, 182 trials after a runtime correction, and the separate 33-trial Nanbeige 32K rerun. The original and continuation rounds show counts because neither alone has three repeats of every task. Nanbeige's full-suite score is withheld because some tasks have no attributable results. Its 12 passes, nine model failures and 12 infrastructure exclusions remain available in the data.

Download the scorecard data and full judging and performance data. The companion preserves all 254 artifact reviews: 247 eligible reviews, six infrastructure diagnostics and one excluded review that exceeded its input limit. Ten attempts produced no artifact to score. The four task rubrics stay separate: the Artifact reviews tables show HTML, code, data and document quality means with eligible artifact counts, plus counts for missing artifacts and excluded reviews. These were unblinded reviews by codex-inline/current-session; no combined quality score is calculated.

The Task performance tables show output and prefill speed, wall time, peak system RSS and peak GPU memory as per-trial medians with ranges and sample counts. They include available observations from all attempts, including failures. These engine-log measurements are separate from admission probes. GPU performance changed during the run, so each journal keeps its own measurement period, including Nanbeige's slower and later faster periods. Download notes explain provenance, exclusions, memory headroom and validation.

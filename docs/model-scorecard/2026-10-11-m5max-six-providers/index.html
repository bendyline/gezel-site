2026-10-11 · Mac · Apple M5 Max

A model scorecard round on M5 Max · Apple M5 Max (integrated GPU) · 128 GB: 4 models across 4 task sets. Every round is listed in the model scorecard, with its results side by side in one table.

M5 Max · Apple M5 Max (integrated GPU) · 128 GB · anthropic + anthropic-cli + codex-cli + mlx + openai · 1 trial per task · 2026-10-11 · gezel 985c646b2e13 · catalog 0.1.94

Compares Claude Sonnet 5.5 and GPT-6 Luna, each reached two ways (through its maker's API and through its command-line app), with two models running on the Mac itself. Generalist mode was on. The run stopped early because of a fault in our test equipment, so a few tasks never ran. Some failures came from our tests or from how gezel connected to the command-line apps, not from the models, and are not counted against them. A model without a result for every task in a set is left out of that table rather than counted as passing. The command-line apps were tested again on October 12 after those problems were fixed.

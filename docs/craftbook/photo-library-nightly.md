# Nightly Photo Albums

The overnight pass over a photo folder. Finds the outings you photographed recently that have no album yet, picks the frames that tell each one, and leaves an album for every outing: a Squisq slideshow with captions and gentle motion that you can play, edit, and export to video. Reads the photo index (capture dates, cameras, captions) instead of opening files, and never moves, copies, renames or deletes a photo. No dated photos yet? It says so and makes nothing up.

## How it runs

| # | Step | Who runs it | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Find the outings | Curator | Recent outings without an album, read from the photo index. |
| 2 | Propose the albums | Curator | One Squisq slideshow per outing: the frames that tell it, captioned, in order. |
| 3 | Finish | Curator | Stamp the summary note and stop. |

Say something like "make albums from my recent photos" or "albums from last weekend" or "nightly photo albums" in chat to start it.

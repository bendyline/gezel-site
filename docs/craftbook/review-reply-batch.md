# Review Reply Batch

Draft replies to every customer review that still needs one, in the business's own voice: each reply thanks the reviewer, answers the specific point they raised, and mentions a fix only when the action list shows it under way. Use after a busy weekend, before a monthly review sweep, or once a CSV of new reviews has been imported.

## How it runs

| # | Step | Who runs it | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Pick the reviews | Researcher | every review in scope that still needs a reply, with the action that answers it |
| 2 | Draft the replies | Copywriter | one reply per review, specific and honest |
| 3 | Finish | Researcher | Stamp a short summary and report DONE. |

Say something like "reply to reviews" or "review replies" or "answer the reviews" or "respond to reviews" or "reply batch" in chat to start it.

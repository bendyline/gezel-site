Structured Interview Kit

Write a structured interview kit for one role from the hiring desk: a job description built on the role's job-related requirements, two questions per requirement with follow-ups, a 1-to-4 scorecard with what each score looks like, and the questions to leave out. Every candidate gets the same questions and is rated on the same evidence; the person hiring makes the decision.

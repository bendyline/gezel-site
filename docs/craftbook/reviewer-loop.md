# Reviewer Loop

Draft → critique → revise (looped until approved) → publish. A repeatable critique-and-revise pattern with explicit branching.

## How it runs

| # | Step | Who runs it | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Draft | Copywriter | Produce the first cut of the requested artifact and make the review target explicit. |
| 2 | Critique | Reviewer | Review the draft against the criteria and route either to revision or publish. |
| 3 | Revise | Copywriter | Address the named critique without redesigning unrelated parts. |
| 4 | Publish | Copywriter | Stamp the final summary after reviewer approval. |

Say something like "draft critique revise" or "reviewer loop" or "critique and revise" or "polish this draft" in chat to start it.

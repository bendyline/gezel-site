Idea Office Hours

Pressure-test a product idea with forcing questions before jumping to solutions, then capture the decision in a short design doc — a structured 'office hours' diagnostic.

How it runs

#StepWho runs itWhat happens
1Frame the opportunityDeveloperTurn the rough idea into a specific problem, audience, outcome, and assumption set.
2Pressure-test the optionsResearcherChallenge the premise and compare meaningful alternatives before choosing a solution.
3Write the decision recordCopywriterCapture a defensible decision and the smallest useful real-world action.
4Evaluate the deliverableReviewerIndependently grade the observable deliverable and route it to finish, repair, or user escalation.
5Repair the deliverableDeveloperFix only the concrete gaps from the latest independent review.
6FinishDeveloperAll deterministic and reviewer criteria passed.
7Escalate unresolved concernsDeveloperThe bounded repair loop ended without a defensible pass.

Say something like "brainstorm this" or "is this worth building" or "help me think through" or "office hours" in chat to start it.

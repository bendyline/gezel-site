# Make sense of a spreadsheet

Hand it a spreadsheet and get back what is in it, what stands out, one table worth keeping, and what to look at next, with every number quoted from the file. Works on a CSV, TSV or Excel file and needs no web access and no code. Use it to make sense of sales figures, budgets, survey exports, sign-up lists, or any other table of numbers.

## How it runs

| # | Step | Who runs it | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Make sense of it | Researcher | Read the spreadsheet and write what is in it and what stands out. |
| 2 | Finish | Researcher | The deliverable passed its checks. Stamp a short summary and report DONE. |

Say something like "make sense of a spreadsheet" or "what stands out in this spreadsheet" or "analyse a spreadsheet" or "analyze a spreadsheet" or "findings from a spreadsheet" or "summarize a csv" in chat to start it.

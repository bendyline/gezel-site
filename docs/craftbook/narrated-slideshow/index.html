Animated Content Slideshow

Turn written content into a real MP4 video and animated GIF with DocBlocks. Locks the story and one message per scene, writes one H1 per scene in slideshow.md, then performs one multi-target conversion, previews the frames, and saves both rendered files in the artifacts drawer — no hand-built HTML player.

How it runs

#StepWho runs itWhat happens
1Outline the scenesPlannerLock the audience, story arc, one message per scene, and media acceptance criteria.
2Write the Markdown slideshowCopywriterWrite slideshow.md with one H1 heading per scene.
3Publish the video and GIFVideo GeneratorConvert slideshow.md once to MP4 and animated GIF, preview frames, and save both files.
4EvaluateReviewerReview the saved MP4 and GIF against the story and visual criteria.
5FinishVideo GeneratorAll story, frame, and media-file checks passed.

Say something like "animated slideshow" or "turn this content into a video" or "make an animated gif from this content" or "content to mp4" or "markdown to video" or "explainer slideshow" or "make a video from slides" in chat to start it.

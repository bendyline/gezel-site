Customer Case Study

Write a persuasive customer/case-study writeup that turns a customer's results into a credible marketing asset — challenge, solution, and quantified results in the classic before/after arc, with a pull-quote and a clear CTA. Scopes the customer story and the hard metrics FIRST (results without numbers are not a case study), writes the narrative copy in the proven challenge-solution-results structure, then builds a clean, branded single-page HTML asset with a results-highlight band and quote callout. Use for marketing case studies, customer success stories, and sales proof assets.

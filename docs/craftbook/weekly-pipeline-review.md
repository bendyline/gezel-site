# Weekly Pipeline Review

The weekly walk of a job-search pipeline — a grounded snapshot of every application (what moved in the last seven days, what went stale, which follow-ups are due), then a review that chases or consciously parks every stalled item and commits to exactly three concrete priorities for next week. Built for the Job Hunt project's weekly cadence; also runs on demand whenever the pipeline feels foggy.

## How it runs

| # | Step | Who runs it | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Snapshot the pipeline | Crew Lead | the grounded state: per-stage counts, movement, staleness, follow-ups due |
| 2 | Write the weekly review | Crew Lead | moved, stalled with unblocking actions, follow-ups, and exactly three priorities |
| 3 | Evaluate | Reviewer | Grade the deliverable against every acceptance criterion. All pass → finish; any fail → loop back and fix the gap. |
| 4 | Finish | Developer | All acceptance criteria met. Stamp a short summary and report DONE. |

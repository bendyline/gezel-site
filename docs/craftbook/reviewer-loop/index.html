Reviewer Loop

Draft → critique → revise (looped until approved) → publish. A repeatable critique-and-revise pattern with explicit branching.

How it runs

#StepWho runs itWhat happens
1DraftCopywriterProduce the first cut of the requested artifact and make the review target explicit.
2CritiqueReviewerReview the draft against the criteria and route either to revision or publish.
3ReviseCopywriterAddress the named critique without redesigning unrelated parts.
4PublishCopywriterStamp the final summary after reviewer approval.

Say something like "draft critique revise" or "reviewer loop" or "critique and revise" or "polish this draft" in chat to start it.

# Weekly Schedule Draft

Draft a week's shift schedule from the roster: every open spot filled from who is available, hours kept inside the limits the business set, the spots nobody can take named plainly, and the whole week left for a person to approve. Use each week before the schedule goes up, or after availability changes.

## How it runs

| # | Step | Who runs it | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Work out the week | Developer | every shift with its open spots, and everyone with their hours and limits |
| 2 | Draft the schedule | Developer | every open spot filled where someone fits every rule, for the person to approve |
| 3 | Finish | Developer | Stamp a short summary and report DONE. |

Say something like "draft the schedule" or "weekly schedule" or "draft next week" or "make the roster" or "schedule draft" in chat to start it.

# Audit Evidence Pack

Assemble an audit-ready evidence pack from a compliance register: every obligation in scope with its owner, due date, status and the proof on file, the gaps ranked by how soon they bite, and the next step for each. Use before an inspection, an insurer's review, a license renewal, or a quarterly 'are we in good standing' check.

## How it runs

| # | Step | Who runs it | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Gather the evidence | Developer | every obligation in scope with its status and the proof on file |
| 2 | Write the pack | Developer | one page an inspector or insurer can read, with the gaps ranked |
| 3 | Finish | Developer | Stamp a short summary and report DONE. |

Say something like "audit pack" or "evidence pack" or "get ready for the inspection" or "are we in good standing" or "compliance pack" in chat to start it.

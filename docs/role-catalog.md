# Every role template

A role template is a ready-made character for your crew: a name, a job, and a working style that shapes how they think and talk. The Meester picks from these when a job calls for one, and you can add any of them yourself with **New crew member**. The core roles each have their own page in [Roles](https://gezel.com/docs/roles-index/).

| Role | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| [Account Manager](https://gezel.com/docs/role-template/account-manager/) | A warm, organized keeper of a small business’s sales: every deal with its stage, value and next follow-up, the calls to make today, and a weighted forecast that stays honest. |
| [Archivist](https://gezel.com/docs/role-template/archivaris/) | A gentle, rigorous archivist for the file nobody wants to need — wills, policies, accounts, instructions. |
| [Bookkeeper](https://gezel.com/docs/role-template/bookkeeper/) | A careful keeper of a small business's books: every receipt booked with its proof, every entry in a category, each month closed on a checklist, and the cash planned weeks ahead. |
| [Builder](https://gezel.com/docs/role-template/builder/) | Takes a job end-to-end on their own — research, build, ship. |
| [Campaign Manager](https://gezel.com/docs/role-template/campaign-manager/) | A steady, numbers-honest runner of one marketing campaign: the goal, the channel calendar, every asset from idea to live, the budget against what was spent, and the results that say what worked. |
| [Care Coordinator](https://gezel.com/docs/role-template/zorgcoordinator/) | A warm, discreet care coordinator. |
| [Career Coach](https://gezel.com/docs/role-template/career-coach/) | A steady career coach who runs your job search like a campaign — keeps the pipeline current, gets your materials tailored, and never lets a follow-up slip. |

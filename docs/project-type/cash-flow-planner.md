# Cash Flow Planner

See the cash coming in and going out, weeks ahead. A bookkeeper keeps a 13-week forecast from the invoices and bills you expect, warns you about the first week cash dips below your cushion, and tries what-ifs such as a late payer or a new hire without touching the plan.

# Vendor & Contract Desk

Keep your vendors and contracts, with their renewal dates, in one place. An office administrator tracks what each contract costs a year, when its term ends, how much notice it needs and whether it renews on its own, and reminds you two weeks before a notice date.

| kantoormeester | Yes — leads the project |
| reviewer | No |

**Plans it installs:** `subscription-audit`, `comparison-matrix`, `contract-template`

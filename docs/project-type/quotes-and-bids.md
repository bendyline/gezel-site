# Quotes & Bids

Price the job and send quotes and bids that win work. An estimator builds each quote line by line from materials and labor at your own rates, keeps every total exact to the cent, follows up on what you sent, and tracks what you win and lose.

**Crew it sets up**

| Role template | Crew lead |
| --- | --- |
| estimator | Yes — leads the project |
| kantoormeester | No |

**Plans it installs:** `proposal-sow`, `invoice-generator`

**Toolsets it suggests:** `docblocks`

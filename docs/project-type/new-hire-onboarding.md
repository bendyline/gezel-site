# New Hire Onboarding

Give each new hire a clear plan for their first weeks. A training coordinator and an office administrator get accounts, equipment and paperwork ready before day one, then walk the first week and the 30, 60 and 90-day check-ins, so nothing is forgotten and nobody starts to an empty desk.

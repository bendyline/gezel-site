Every project type

A project type outfits a whole project for one kind of work: the right crew, craftbooks, toolsets, and schedules, composed into a single installable setup.

Browse Project types in the Gezel Gilde for the public catalog view.

Project typeWhat it sets up
Caregiving BinderCoordinate a loved one's care without losing the thread.
CheckersPlay checkers against a gezel opponent on a live board.
ChessPlay a complete game of chess against a gezel opponent on a live board.
Design SchemeExplore color schemes and design directions with a designer.
Email / InboxSync an email account into a project as canonical markdown, then triage, draft, and summarize correspondence with a gezel.
Event PlannerPlan the wedding, the milestone birthday, the reunion.
Fitness CoachA training habit that survives real life.
FlashcardsLearn anything with spaced-repetition flashcards.
Freelance OfficeRun a one-person business without dropping anything.
Fundraiser HQRally a community drive.
GoPlay Go against a gezel opponent on a live 9×9, 13×13, or 19×19 board.
Household BudgetKnow where the money actually goes.
Household ManualThe living manual for your home.
Image FeedA visual feed with a working rhythm — your Omroeper runs the posting pipeline, an image maker turns notions into pictures in the feed's own style, and a live gallery shows every post from first idea to published.
Job HuntRun your job search like a campaign — a career coach tracks every application through the pipeline, a mock interviewer gets you ready, and a live board keeps the whole slog navigable.
Just ChatA quiet room for talking — no tasks, no workspace, no deliverables.
Language TrainerPractice a new language by chatting with a patient tutor who corrects gently and tracks your progress.
Life BinderThe "in case of emergency" file, actually complete.
Meal Planner & PantryAnswer "what's for dinner?" once a week.
Novel Writing RoomA room for the long haul of a novel.
Social FeedRun a social feed like a small newsroom — an Omroeper keeps every post moving through a real pipeline (idea to published, nothing by accident), a copywriter sharpens each variant for its platform, and a live board shows exactly where every post stands.
Software ProjectA working codebase with a real crew: a developer voorman who builds and fixes, a reviewer who checks the work, and a boekwachter who reads the code while you sleep and files what it finds.
Trip PlannerPlan a trip you'll actually be present for.

What’s inside

The crew

  • Fundraiser Crew leadAn infectious, organized rallier for community drives.

Plans it follows

  • Social Media ThreadWrite a multi-post social thread (X/Twitter, LinkedIn, Threads) that hooks, delivers, and converts: it first locks the single angle/big idea, the platform and its character limits, the target reader, and the CTA, then drafts the thread as numbered posts with a scroll-stopping hook, one idea per post, and a clear close.
  • Fundraiser Wrap-UpClose a fundraising drive properly: verify the final numbers against the donation ledger, confirm every donor was thanked (or finish the thank-yous now), and write the wrap-up story — the total, what the money does, and the people who made it happen — ready to share with the community and hand to the next organizer.

Actions Gezel can take

  • Record donationRecord a gift the moment it lands (ordinary units, donor name).
  • Mark thankedRecord that a thank-you went out for a gift.
  • Drive statusThe thermometer and the thank-you debt.
  • Donor listEvery gift, with thank-you state.

Each action is a small script that changes the app’s files, checked by Gezel’s security settings before it runs.

Files it keeps

Plain files in the project folder you can open, back up, and read: donations.json.

Start one

  1. Download Gezel for Mac, Windows or Linux. It’s free.
  2. Choose New project, then Events & Journeys, then Fundraiser HQ.
  3. Answer a few questions: What are you raising for? Goal (in cents)
  4. Your fundraiser takes it from there, on the model you choose.

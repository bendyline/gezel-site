Image Feed

A visual feed with a working rhythm — your social media writer runs the posting pipeline, an image maker turns notions into pictures in the feed's own style, and a live gallery shows every post from first idea to published.

What’s inside

The crew

  • Social Media Writer Crew leadThe town crier for your feeds.
  • Image generatorAn image generator.

Plans it follows

  • Draft a Social PostDraft one social post from idea to approved copy.
  • Social DigestThe overnight read of your social feeds.
  • Reception ReportThe weekly reception review for your social posting.

Runs Social Digest overnight, once you agree. Runs Reception Report on a schedule, once you agree.

Actions Gezel can take

  • List postsSee the whole post pipeline: every post with its status, platforms, and dates.
  • Get postRead one post in full: frontmatter, body, each platform variant with its character count (and permalink once live), and media files.
  • Create postCommit the staged posts/_drafting/ folder as a real post.
  • Update postPatch a post's frontmatter (fields object; null clears a key) and/or move it one pipeline stage.
  • Record publicationThe moment a variant goes live, stamp it with its permalink and platform post id.

Each action is a small script that changes the app’s files, checked by Gezel’s security settings before it runs.

Files it keeps

Plain files in the project folder you can open, back up, and read: style-guide.md, posts/index.json.

Start one

  1. Download Gezel for Mac, Windows or Linux. It’s free.
  2. Choose New project, then Photos & Media, then Image Feed.
  3. Answer a few questions: Who does this feed speak for? Instagram handle Visual style
  4. Your social media writer takes it from there, on the model you choose.

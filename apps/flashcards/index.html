Flashcards

Learn anything with spaced-repetition flashcards. Your study partner writes the cards in chat; you review them on a live card-flip page that schedules what comes back and when.

Try it

Open in a full window ↗

Flashcards running in your browser on sample data. Use the page, or talk with the study partner beside it: the conversation is scripted for this preview, and the changes stay in the page.

What’s inside

The crew

  • Study Partner Crew leadA patient study companion who writes sharp flashcards, keeps the review rhythm going, and cheers the streak without ever shaming a miss.

Actions the crew can take

  • Add cardsAdd flashcards to the deck: a cards array of {front, back} objects, one fact per card, at most 8 at a time.
  • Deck statusSee the deck: how many cards, how many due, and the box distribution.
  • List deckRead every card in the deck (id, box, front, back).

Each action is a small script that changes the app’s files, checked by Gezel’s security settings before it runs.

Files it keeps

Plain files in the project folder you can open, back up, and read: deck.json.

Start one

  1. Download Gezel for Mac, Windows or Linux. It’s free.
  2. Choose New project, then Personal Growth, then Flashcards.
  3. Answer its question: What are you learning?
  4. Your study partner takes it from there, on the model you choose.

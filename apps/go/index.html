Go

Play Go against a gezel opponent on a live 9×9, 13×13, or 19×19 board. Place black stones; the Go player reads the exact position, replies in white, and keeps the conversation calm and sporting.

Try it

Open in a full window ↗

Go running in your browser on sample data. Use the page, or talk with the go player beside it: the conversation is scripted for this preview, and the changes stay in the page.

What’s inside

The crew

  • Go Player Crew leadA calm, attentive Go opponent who plays legal stones, respects the quiet shape of the board, and handles captures, passes, and final scores with good grace.

Actions Gezel can take

  • Get boardSee the authoritative Go position, board size, captures, whose turn it is, and every legal move.
  • Make movePlay your white move.

Each action is a small script that changes the app’s files, checked by Gezel’s security settings before it runs.

Files it keeps

Plain files in the project folder you can open, back up, and read: game.json.

Start one

  1. Download Gezel for Mac, Windows or Linux. It’s free.
  2. Choose New project, then Games, then Go.
  3. Answer a few questions: Table-talk style How should the Go player play?
  4. Your go player takes it from there, on the model you choose.

Household Manual

The living manual for your home. A home keeper keeps a card for every system — what care it needs and when — turns problems into flagged history, and the dashboard shows what the house needs this season.

What’s inside

The crew

  • Home Keeper Crew leadAn unflappable house steward.

Plans it follows

  • Seasonal Maintenance SweepRun a season's home-care sweep against the project's systems roster: work out what care is due or overdue from each system's interval, plan the walkthrough in a sensible order with supplies, then log the completed care and flag what needs a professional — leaving the roster's due-tracking reset and a short sweep report.

Actions Gezel can take

  • Record systemAdd a system or appliance card (with a care interval in days when known).
  • Log maintenanceLog that care happened (resets the due clock).
  • Flag attentionFlag a problem on a system (a noise, a drip, a trip).
  • House statusWhat the house needs: care due and flagged problems.
  • House listRead every system card.

Each action is a small script that changes the app’s files, checked by Gezel’s security settings before it runs.

Files it keeps

Plain files in the project folder you can open, back up, and read: systems.json.

Start one

  1. Download Gezel for Mac, Windows or Linux. It’s free.
  2. Choose New project, then Home & Family, then Household Manual.
  3. Answer its question: What kind of home?
  4. Your home keeper takes it from there, on the model you choose.
